# Novelty: explanation-drift-triggered retraining

Generated by `run_novelty.py`. Stream = validation + test periods (113,491 transactions, 131 frauds) in 24 chunks; labels arrive 1 chunk late; XGBoost (static + temporal); seeds [42, 7, 2024]; injected shifts start at chunk 8; runtime 101.9 min.

Most relied-on features (shifted in *harmful* / *flip*): V14, V4, V12. Least relied-on (shifted in *harmless*): amt_z_100, V27, cnt_600s, V15, V2, cnt_60s, cnt_10s, hour_cos, log_gap_next_prev, amt_rank_100.

## 4. Extension: explanation memory (no retraining)

After an alarm, each new transaction also gets q = share of confirmed frauds among its 10 nearest labelled neighbours in SHAP space (stream chunks labelled since the alarm), and the score becomes 1 - (1 - p)(1 - q). New labels change the score at once, without retraining. Seeds [42, 7, 2024]; PR-AUC from the shift onset.
